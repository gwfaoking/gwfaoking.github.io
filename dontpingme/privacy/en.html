don't ping me! Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

The developer of don't ping me! (“the app”) processes your information as described below, in line with Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act and, for users in the EU/EEA and UK, the GDPR. All processing happens on your device; the developer runs no servers and does not collect or receive your personal or location information.

1. Purposes

  • Keeping, holding or removing other apps’ alerts according to the policies you set (apps, text, time, situation conditions)
  • Alert history, Inbox and missed-alert summaries
  • Evaluating Wi‑Fi (connected or nearby), Bluetooth, Android Auto and place conditions (only if you add them)
  • Diagnostics (each app’s last-received time and error)

3. Retention

  • Alert history and Inbox: deleted after the period you choose (1, 3, 7, 30, 90, 180 days or no limit; 30 days by default); when the storage you choose (100 MB–1 GB, 100 MB by default) is full, the oldest items go first.
  • Internal processing log: latest 400 entries.
  • Policies and conditions: until you delete them or the app.
  • Current location: discarded right after each check, never recorded. Only inside/outside status and the names of the last few Wi‑Fi connections are kept.
  • Names of Wi‑Fi networks seen nearby and when they were seen: kept only in memory while the app runs, never stored, and ignored after 3 minutes.
  • Consent records: until the app is uninstalled.

4. Deletion procedure

Expired or deleted data is removed from the app’s private storage immediately. Uninstalling the app makes Android delete all its private data. Backup files you export are managed by you.

5. Third parties and processors

The developer does not share your information with third parties or use processors.

7. Your rights

You can exercise these rights in the app at any time: access (history, Inbox, policies), rectification and erasure (edit/delete policies, Settings › Clear history), restriction (turn off alert control or notification access), withdrawal of consent (Settings › About › Consent history: withdraw location consent, or withdraw all and delete all data), and portability (settings backup as JSON). Because the developer holds none of your data, there is nothing to request from the developer; questions are welcome at the contact below.

GDPR (EU/EEA/UK): The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) and, for core alert handling you request, performance of the service (Art. 6(1)(b)). You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing, and you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The app is for users aged 14 or older (16 or older in the EU/EEA). Age is confirmed on first use; children under 14 may not use it.

9. Security

  • All data stays in Android’s app-private storage, unreadable by other apps.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer; map images are fetched over HTTPS.
  • Permissions are requested only when you first use the feature that needs them.

10. Automatic collection

No cookies, advertising IDs, analytics or tracking tools are used.

11. Location information

Location is used only with your separate in-app consent and only on your device to evaluate Wi‑Fi and place conditions. Your current location is discarded right after each check; it is never sent to the developer, stored on a server or shared with third parties.

13. Backup

  • A settings backup saves only the items you choose (policies, app settings, Inbox, alert history; policies and app settings by default) as a JSON file where you choose, which may create a copy off the device.
  • App data is not included in Android automatic backup (Google Drive or other cloud); it is excluded so alert contents and place coordinates don’t go to servers abroad.
  • A direct device-to-device transfer to a new phone (no server involved) may move app data to the new device.

14. Paid features

When you buy Pro features, Google Play handles the payment; the app stores only the information needed to confirm the purchase (purchase token and product) on your device. The developer never receives card numbers or other payment details. Through Google Play Console the developer can see order details such as order number, product, purchase time and country, used only to confirm orders and process refunds and kept only as long as the law requires. Payment processing follows Google’s privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
